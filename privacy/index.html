Privacy policy

ChamberSathi is a single-device case management app for advocates. Last updated 17 August 2026.

There is no ChamberSathi user account and no ChamberSathi cloud sync backend in this version. We do not operate a server that receives your case contents.

1. Who we are

ChamberSathi (“we”) publishes the Android app ChamberSathi (package com.chambersathi.app). This policy describes how the app handles information on your device and the limited cases where data may leave the device.

Contact: chambersathi@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

The following stay in an encrypted local database (SQLCipher) and the app’s sandboxed file storage on your phone:

  • Profile, clients, cases, hearings, tasks, events, documents, and payments
  • App unlock PIN (stored as a salted hash) and related security settings
  • Local encrypted backups you create (you choose where to save the file)

The database encryption key is kept in the OS secure keystore (via the platform secure storage API), not in a plain file you can copy. Unlocking the UI with PIN or biometrics does not send those secrets to ChamberSathi.

3. Data that may leave the device

Channel What Why
OS share sheet (you initiate) Encrypted .csbak backup, or an unencrypted JSON export of clients, cases, hearings, tasks, events, and payments You choose where the file goes (Files, Drive, email, USB). ChamberSathi does not upload it.
Google Play Billing Purchase / restore of the Pro subscription (Rs. 599 per year) Process the store transaction on this Android app
Firebase Crashlytics (optional) Anonymous crash and diagnostic metadata (for example app version, build channel, non-PII feature flags such as whether unlimited-cases is entitled) Improve app stability

Crash reporting is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy → Share crash reports. Crash reports must not include client names, phone numbers, emails, CNRs, case notes, document file names, backup passphrases, PIN/biometric secrets, or raw license/receipt payloads.

If Crashlytics or Play Billing is unreachable (for example airplane mode), core app features continue to work offline. Crash reports may queue and upload later when a connection is available.

4. What we do not do (v1)

  • No ChamberSathi account or login
  • No cloud backup or multi-device sync of case data
  • No advertising SDK
  • No Firebase Auth, Firestore, Storage, Remote Config, or Analytics for product features
  • Pro unlock on Android via Google Play does not unlock a Windows product

5. Your choices

  • Turn off crash reporting anytime: Settings → Privacy → Share crash reports
  • Create or restore an encrypted local backup without involving ChamberSathi servers
  • Export an unencrypted JSON copy of chamber records (Settings → Export JSON) if you want to leave for another app — this file is not encrypted; do not share it casually
  • Wipe local data from the app (destructive) if you want a clean start

6. Data retention

Practice data remains on the device until you delete records, wipe the app, uninstall, or restore over it from a backup. We do not hold a copy of your chamber on our servers. Google Play and Firebase retain purchase and crash records under their own policies for the limited data described above.

7. Children

ChamberSathi is built for practising advocates and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-party services

Google Play Billing and (if you leave crash reporting on) Google Firebase Crashlytics process the data in section 3 under Google’s terms. ChamberSathi does not sell your information and does not use case contents for advertising.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes that affect Play Data safety will be reflected in the Google Play listing as well.

10. Contact
